DuctTape for Mac: what's in this download besides DuctTape

BepInEx 5.4.23.5 (MIT, LICENSE-BepInEx.txt), https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx
  BepInEx/core. BepInEx.dll and BepInEx.Preloader.dll are built, unmodified, from the v5-lts source at f4c1b11, which

UnityDoorstop 4.6.0 (LGPL-2.1, LICENSE-UnityDoorstop.txt), https://github.com/NeighTools/UnityDoorstop
  libdoorstop.dylib, unmodified from the CI release. run_bepinex.sh is BepInEx 5.4.23.5's script with the game's
  name filled in (executable_name="ROUNDS.app").
