# Changelog

## 1.1.1

UnboundLib 3.2.14 now becomes our fork of Bknibb's UnboundLib 4.2.7 (was his 4.2.5), with fixes for the current
game: Escape goes back a page in the escape menu's MODS pages again, Toggle Cards and Toggle Levels open in front of
the escape menu and the map (they opened behind them), a rematch no longer gets stuck on a card that's already gone,
and it works on a Mac without a separate fix. No "has an update
available!" line on the main menu for the UnboundLib and RoundsWithFriends DuctTape makes, since a mod manager can't
update them. Cards built from an asset bundle (RSCards and others) show their descriptions again. AutoFix 1.5.1,
Runtime 1.6.0.

## 1.1.0

The old UnboundLib 3.2.14, MMHook 1.0.0 and RoundsWithFriends 2.2.2 your mods depend on are patched into Bknibb's
ports (UnboundLib 4.2.5, RoundsWithFriends 3.0.10) every start: nothing extra to install. AutoFix 1.5.0.

## 1.0.1

Runtime 1.5.0: LobbyImprovements' lobby code box is built again (the current game's TextMeshPro threw when asked for
the font of a text that hadn't been drawn yet).

## 1.0.0

First release: AutoFix 1.4.0 (libraries, mod fixes, hand-made fixes), Runtime 1.4.0 (in-game fixes), Odin Serializer.
Bknibb's UnboundLib 4 and RoundsWithFriends 3 are installed alongside it; DuctTape downloads nothing.
